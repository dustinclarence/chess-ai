    How the AI thinks
    1. It lists every legal move for the position.
    2. For each move it imagines the replies, then the replies to those, down to the chosen depth (minimax).
    3. At the end of each line it scores the position: material (pawn 1, knight 3.2, bishop 3.3, rook 5, queen 9) plus piece-square tables that reward good squares, such as knights in the centre.
    4. Alpha-beta pruning skips lines that cannot change the decision, and captures are searched first so pruning works better.
    5. It plays the move with the best guaranteed score.

    Rules included: castling, en passant, promotion (to a queen), check, checkmate and stalemate.
